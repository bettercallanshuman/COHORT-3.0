The Weeknd

Abel Makkonen Tesfaye, known professionally as The Weeknd, is a Canadian singer-songwriter and record producer.

1. Early Life & Career

He was born on February 16, 1990, in Toronto, Ontario. He began his career in 2009 by anonymously releasing music on YouTube.

His dark, alternative R&B style helped him gain a massive following. He is heavily influenced by Michael Jackson and Prince.

His album Starboy went #1 on the Billboard 200 chart.
